# Usage Ledger v0.2.52

- Show one reading in the account panel for each confirmed Codex quota window and cycle. Other readings remain available under Quota sources & status with their percentages and timestamps. If a new reading is unavailable, show the last valid reading as such.
- Keep the last measured output rate during brief gaps while a tool is running. Clearly distinguish current rates, 60-second and five-minute averages, and previous readings.
- Simplify the account quota panel and its English and Chinese labels. Keep account costs in their existing tab and remove the duplicate shortcut.
- Add built-in official reference pricing for [GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), including the API long-context rules and the time-limited Ultrafast rate.
- Add offline reference pricing for [Claude Fable 5.2](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/whats-new-fable-5-2), including Fast, Batch, and both cache-write durations.

The ledger fills in missing quotes without overwriting saved quotes, payment records, or quota history. Subscription usage estimates are not actual charges.
